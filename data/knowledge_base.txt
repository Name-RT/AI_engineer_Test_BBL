=== Section 1: International Travel Policy ===
All employees traveling internationally for business purposes must obtain approval from their department head and the HR department at least 30 days prior to their planned departure. This advance notice ensures that all arrangements, including visas and accommodations, are processed in a timely manner. The company covers allowed expenses such as flights (economy class), lodging, and meals based on established per diem rates for the destination country. Employees must use the company-approved booking portal for all reservations. Furthermore, the company provides comprehensive travel insurance for the duration of the trip. To process reimbursements, employees are required to submit all original receipts, a detailed itinerary, and a post-travel report within 14 days of returning. Failure to provide required documentation may result in denied reimbursement.

=== Section 2: Remote Work Policy ===
The company supports a flexible work environment. Employees who have completed their initial 90-day probationary period are eligible to request remote work arrangements. Approved employees may work remotely for a maximum of 3 days per week. All remote workers are required to be online and available during the core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone. The company will provide essential equipment, including a laptop and secure VPN access, which must be used for all corporate network connections. Employees are expected to maintain the same level of performance and responsiveness as they would in the office. Any technical issues preventing work must be reported immediately to the IT helpdesk.

=== Section 3: Annual Leave Policy ===
Employees accrue annual leave based on their tenure with the company. The standard accrual rate is 1.5 days per month for the first three years of service. A maximum of 5 days of unused annual leave can be carried over to the following calendar year. Employees must note that there are blackout periods, specifically during the last two weeks of the fiscal year and major product launches, where leave requests will generally be denied unless in exceptional circumstances. All leave requests must follow the approval process, requiring sign-off from the direct manager with a minimum notice of 14 days. In cases of unforeseen personal emergencies, emergency leave can be requested with immediate notice, subject to HR review.

=== Section 4: Expense Reimbursement Policy ===
This policy outlines the procedures for claiming business-related expenses. Eligible expenses include client entertainment, business travel, and approved office supplies. Employees must provide itemized receipt requirements for any expense exceeding $25. All reimbursement claims must meet the submission deadline of 30 days from the date the expense was incurred. The approval process follows a tiered system: managers can approve expenses up to $500, while amounts above $500 require director-level approval. For frequent travelers and department heads, corporate credit card rules apply, mandating that the card be used strictly for business purposes and that all statements be reconciled monthly. Personal expenses on corporate cards are strictly prohibited.

=== Section 5: Code of Conduct ===
Our Code of Conduct defines the expectations for professional behavior within the workplace. All employees must act with integrity and respect towards colleagues, clients, and partners. Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately. The gifts policy strictly limits the acceptance of gifts from clients or vendors to a value of $50 or less, and cash gifts are never permitted. Employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding company data, intellectual property, and client information. Our social media guidelines require employees to clearly state that their views are their own and not representative of the company. Any employee witnessing violations is encouraged to use the anonymous reporting violations hotline.

=== Section 6: IT Security Policy ===
Maintaining the security of company data is a shared responsibility. Strict password requirements are enforced, including a minimum of 12 characters, a mix of character types, and mandatory changes every 90 days. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for accessing all company systems and emails. Our device management protocol requires all corporate devices to have MDM (Mobile Device Management) software installed to allow remote wiping in case of loss. Data classification rules dictate that highly sensitive data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Employees must familiarize themselves with incident reporting procedures and immediately notify IT of any suspected phishing attempts or security breaches. Only approved software from the company catalog may be installed on work devices.

=== Section 7: Data Privacy & Personal Data Protection Policy (PDPA) ===
The organization is fully committed to compliance with Personal Data Protection regulations (including PDPA and international privacy standards). All personal data belonging to customers, employees, and third-party vendors must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently for explicit corporate purposes only. Consent must be documented prior to collecting sensitive personal records. Data minimization principles apply across all departments: staff may only access personal information strictly necessary for their specific job duties. In the event of an actual or suspected data breach, employees must report the incident to the Data Protection Officer (DPO) immediately; the organization is legally mandated to notify relevant regulatory authorities within 72 hours. Unauthorized disclosure or mishandling of customer data will result in disciplinary action up to termination and legal prosecution.

=== Section 8: Health, Wellness, Sick Leave & Medical Benefits Policy ===
The company provides comprehensive medical and wellness coverage to support employee well-being. All full-time employees and their eligible dependents are enrolled in the Group Health Insurance Plan upon completion of their first month of employment. The coverage includes inpatient hospitalization (IPD), outpatient consultations (OPD) up to 30 visits per year, and annual dental allowances of up to $500. A complimentary comprehensive annual health check-up is provided at designated partner hospitals each calendar year. In addition, employees are entitled to up to 30 days of paid Sick Leave per calendar year for legitimate illness or medical recovery. For any sick leave duration exceeding 3 consecutive working days, employees must submit a certified medical certificate from a licensed physician via the HR portal within 48 hours of returning to work. For immediate illness, staff must inform their direct supervisor prior to 9:00 AM on the day of absence. Additionally, the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers confidential 24/7 mental health counseling, stress management sessions, and financial advisory services at zero cost to the employee. Parental leave provisions grant 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.

=== Section 9: Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy ===
As a regulated financial institution, the bank enforces strict Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Terrorism Financing (CTF), and Know Your Customer (KYC) protocols. Employees interacting with client accounts must conduct thorough Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk accounts, politically exposed persons (PEPs), and complex corporate entities. All transactions exceeding regulatory cash thresholds or demonstrating unusual patterns must be flagged and submitted to the Compliance Department via a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) within 24 hours. Tipping off clients regarding AML investigations is a severe criminal offense. Mandatory annual AML certification training must be completed by all staff before the end of Q2 each year.

=== Section 10: Performance Management & Promotion Policy ===
Our performance management framework operates on a bi-annual review cycle (Mid-Year Review in July and Year-End Evaluation in December). Employee performance is evaluated against Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and core organizational competencies utilizing a 360-degree feedback model. Performance ratings range from 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exemplary). Employees achieving consistent ratings of 4 or above over two consecutive cycles are eligible for merit-based promotions and salary adjustments. For employees receiving an Unsatisfactory rating, a structured 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) will be established with clear milestones; failure to meet PIP objectives may lead to reassignment or contract termination.

=== Section 11: Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Procedures Policy ===
The company is committed to maintaining a safe, hazard-free physical and psychological work environment. All office facilities are equipped with automated external defibrillators (AEDs), first-aid stations, and certified emergency evacuation routes. Mandatory fire and safety evacuation drills are conducted bi-annually. Any workplace accident, injury, or near-miss incident must be reported to the Health & Safety Officer within 24 hours using the Incident Log Portal. Ergonomic workstation evaluations and equipment adjustments (such as adjustable monitor arms and ergonomic chairs) can be requested through Facilities Management. Smoking, vaping, and possession of alcohol or illicit substances on company premises are strictly prohibited.

=== Section 12: Whistleblowing & Grievance Policy ===
The bank fosters a culture of transparency, accountability, and ethical governance. The Whistleblowing Policy provides safe, confidential, and independent channels for employees and stakeholders to report suspected illegal activities, fraud, corruption, bribery, discrimination, or severe policy breaches. Reports can be submitted 24/7 via the encrypted Whistleblowing Portal or the dedicated toll-free telephone hotline. The organization strictly enforces a Zero-Retaliation Guarantee: any retaliatory action against a whistleblower acting in good faith is treated as gross misconduct resulting in immediate dismissal. All reports are independently investigated by the Internal Audit and Ethics Committee within 30 business days.

=== Section 13: Intellectual Property & Inventions Policy ===
All inventions, software code, algorithms, research findings, technical designs, trade secrets, and documentation created by employees during the course of their employment belong exclusively to the company. Employees agree to assign all worldwide intellectual property rights, patents, trademarks, and copyrights to the organization upon creation. Prior to incorporating any open-source software (OSS) or third-party libraries into corporate software systems, engineers must obtain formal approval from the Architecture and Legal Review Board. Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) remain legally binding during and indefinitely after the termination of employment, prohibiting the transfer or retention of proprietary source code or confidential datasets.

=== Section 14: Learning, Training & Professional Development Policy ===
To encourage continuous skill enhancement, full-time employees are allocated an individual annual Learning & Development budget of up to $1,500 for professional courses, conferences, and technical certifications relevant to their role. Reimbursement is processed upon submission of proof of completion and valid invoices. The company also supports advanced degree programs through approved educational assistance; employees receiving academic sponsorships exceeding $5,000 must sign a 12-month retention bond upon course graduation. In addition, each employee is entitled to up to 3 days of paid Study Leave per calendar year to sit for recognized professional examinations.

=== Section 15: Severance, Resignation & Offboarding Policy ===
Employees wishing to resign must submit a formal written notice to their direct manager and HR. The standard notice period is 30 days for operational staff and 60 days for managerial or executive positions. During the notice period, employees must ensure a thorough handover of duties, projects, and client accounts documented in a formal Handover Sign-off Form. All company property, including laptops, access badges, security tokens, and corporate credit cards, must be surrendered to IT and Facilities on or before the final working day. Severance pay for involuntary separations is calculated strictly in accordance with national labor law based on completed years of service. Final salary, prorated unused leave compensation, and employment certificates will be disbursed within 7 business days following exit clearance.\n
